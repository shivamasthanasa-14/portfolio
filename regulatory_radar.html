Sources and methodology note: Compiled from EU Official Journal texts, European Commission implementation pages, and current legal and compliance analysis (DEHSt, EY, ICAP, Consilium, Hogan Lovells, Anthesis, Assent, Coolset, CIRS Group, AEM, and others), current as of August 2026. Industry relevance ratings reflect each regulation's own defined legal scope (named CBAM sectors, EUDR commodities, CPR and ESPR priority product categories) cross-referenced with sector-specific compliance guidance, not a formal scoring model. Steel, Automotive, Chemicals, Packaging, Machinery, Food & Beverage, and Building Materials entries are based on dedicated industry-specific research. Electronics is the one industry where a real gap remains: RoHS and WEEE, arguably the two most consequential regulations for that sector day to day, are not among the 10 tracked here and would need to be added for this filter to be genuinely complete for that industry. Status and dates on several regulations, particularly EUDR and CSDDD, have shifted more than once and may shift again. This is a general tracking framework, not advice specific to any employer or product scope.
